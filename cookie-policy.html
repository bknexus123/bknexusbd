Cookie Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This policy describes the browser storage, tags, and similar technologies used on bknexusbd.com.

1. Essential storage

The website stores your cookie preference in your browser under bk_nexus_cookie_consent_v1. This is necessary to remember whether you accepted or rejected optional technologies. It does not identify you directly and remains until you clear browser storage or change your preference.

2. Optional analytics and marketing technologies

Google Tag Manager container GTM-TMPS857Z is loaded only after you choose “Accept optional.” Tag Manager is a tag-delivery system. The precise cookies and retention periods depend on the analytics or marketing tags BK Nexus configures inside the container. Any future GA4, Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag, or similar optional tag must remain governed by the consent choice and this policy must be updated before or when it is activated.

3. Your choices

You may accept or reject optional technologies with equal ease. Rejection does not prevent access to the website or the project inquiry form. Use Cookie Settings at any time to make a new choice. You may also clear your browser storage.

4. Project form

The inquiry form uses FormSubmit only when you actively submit the form. It is not an advertising cookie. Please review the Privacy Policy for information about form data processing.

5. Contact

Questions about cookies or privacy can be sent to info@bknexusbd.com.
