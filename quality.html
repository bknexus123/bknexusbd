Quality & Compliance

Quality begins before production.

Our quality process is designed to protect the approved product through clear standards, early risk control, production checkpoints, and final verification.

Quality Control Process

A visible, disciplined path from approval to delivery.

Quality is not added at the end. It is established through product understanding, material decisions, approved standards, and control during production.

01

Risk Review

Construction, material, measurements, and critical quality points are reviewed early.

02

Approval Standard

Samples, specifications, color, trims, and workmanship expectations are aligned.

03

Pre-Production

Production readiness, patterns, size sets, and approved comments are verified.

04

In-Line Quality

Measurements, construction, defects, and process consistency are monitored.

05

Final Inspection

Finished goods, packing, assortment, and required quality level are reviewed.

06

Corrective Action

Issues are documented, corrected, and followed through before release.

Continuous Improvement

Better outcomes come from learning across every program.

Quality improvement means using inspection findings, production feedback, customer comments, and root-cause analysis to prevent repeat issues.

01

Root-Cause Review

We look beyond the visible defect to identify the process that allowed it.

02

Corrective & Preventive Action

Actions are defined, assigned, and monitored to reduce recurrence.

03

Performance Feedback

Quality findings are shared with relevant production teams for improvement.

04

Program Learning

Knowledge from one order is carried into the next development and production cycle.
